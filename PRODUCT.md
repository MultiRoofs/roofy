## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Roofy is a browser-based viewer and analysis workspace for 3D city models supporting MultiRoofs. It focuses on rooftop analysis for planning, simulation and communication.

## Users

People inspecting city models and rooftop information for planning and research. Prioritization among these audiences remains open.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Load city-model and geospatial layers; inspect features and roof surfaces.
- Style and filter layers, inspect records and calculate summaries.
- Explore sun position and shading through date, time and scene controls.
- Save local workspaces and share supported views through URLs; no login in v1.
- Browser-first architecture; separate ingestion, rendering, analysis, persistence and UI.
- Use citymodel for the domain; CityJSON, CityJSONSeq and FlatCityBuf are encodings.
- The current map workspace retains a 1024px desktop minimum width.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the Roofy name and four-plane mark. The user requested professional, minimal typography throughout the map workspace, restrained button shapes, greater separation between major sections, compact detailed controls, and contained panels.
